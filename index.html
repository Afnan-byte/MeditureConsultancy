DHA
The Dubai Health Authority (DHA) mandates that healthcare professionals intend to work in Dubai. The Dataflow procedure undergoes exam registration, Registration( Credential), Dataflow(Verification). This process, managed by the DHA Data Flow Group, involves verifying the authenticity of educational degrees, work experience, and professional licenses.
